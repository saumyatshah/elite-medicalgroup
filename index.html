The Practice Roots

Board-certified physicians, with time to listen.

Since 2009, Dr. Parth Shah has looked after patients across Montgomery County, Christian County, and nearby communities. Dr. Pankaj Shah, a board-certified internist, began practicing in 2007, and his work takes him from the exam room to the hospital floor and the intensive care unit.

Elite Medical Group brings that experience together in one primary care practice, where you can keep seeing doctors who know your history.

Dr. Parth Shah MD

M.D. · Board-Certified, Family Medicine

For more than seventeen years, Dr. Parth has treated patients through their teens, adulthood, and older age. His experience includes adolescent care, acute illness, wound care, and geriatric care.

He takes his time in every visit, and he believes patients do best with a doctor who plans to stay in the community for years to come.

Partner · Internal Medicine
Partner Physician

Dr. Pankaj Shah MD

M.D. · Board-Certified, Internal Medicine

Dr. Pankaj is a board-certified internist who treats adults in the clinic, in the hospital, and in the intensive care unit. He focuses on complex adult medicine, including illness that affects several body systems, long-term conditions, and patients at their sickest.

Patients say he listens closely and explains things in plain language, and he works hand in hand with families and specialists.
