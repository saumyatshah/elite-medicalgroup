The Practice Roots

A team of board certified physicians. A single, unhurried practice.

Dr. Parth has practiced family medicine since 2009, caring for patients across Montgomery and Christian County and the surrounding communities. Dr. Pankaj, board-certified in internal medicine, has practiced since 2007 — with a clinical range that spans the office, the hospital, and the intensive care unit.

Elite Medical Group is the home for that combined experience: a physician-owned primary care practice rooted in continuity, built for the long relationships that good medicine depends on.
